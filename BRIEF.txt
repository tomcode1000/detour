------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A company's employee is travelling. Their flight is cancelled or badly
delayed. Detour notices, and within a minute the employee has a virtual
card in the local currency that works only for food, a hotel and taxis,
holds the right amount for that city, and expires when they get home.
As the disruption changes, Detour changes the card.

Lifecycle of one disruption:
  1. Watch     Trip on file (flights, traveller, home currency). Flight
               status feed + airline notification emails.
  2. Detect    Cancelled, or delay past threshold, or missed connection.
  3. Fund      Issue a virtual card: meals + taxis, city-rate limits,
               active until the rebooked departure + 6h.
  4. Adapt     Delay becomes overnight -> add hotel categories, raise the
               limit. Rebooked -> move the expiry. Employee asks for more
               ("hotel wants a 150 EUR deposit") -> policy check -> raise
               or escalate.
  5. Escalate  Anything past policy (2nd night, rate above cap) goes to a
               travel manager with the amount, currency, merchant type and
               evidence bound to the approval.
  6. Close     Employee lands -> card set INACTIVE then CLOSED. Spend
               summary from card transactions (no receipts). EU261 claim
               pack drafted where it applies.

One-line pitch:
  Today a stranded employee pays out of pocket and waits weeks to be
  repaid. With Detour the money is there before they have to ask, and it
  can only be spent on getting them home.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PROBLEM (plain words)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A cancelled flight at 11pm leaves the traveller paying for a hotel,
  food and taxis on a personal card, keeping receipts, filing an expense
  claim, and waiting to be reimbursed.
- The company has a duty of care but no view of what is happening or
  being spent until the expense report lands.
- Big players prove the need: U.S. Bank built time-limited virtual cards
  that airlines push to stranded passengers (Card as a Service, 2021) and
  deactivate when they board; Edenred sells virtual cards issued
  automatically on flight-delay triggers to insurers/assistance firms.
- Nobody offers this to the small and mid-size companies that Airwallex
  serves. They have cards and travel bookings, but nothing joins the two
  when a trip breaks.
- In the EU the airline owes "care" (meals, hotel) and often 250-600 EUR
  compensation (EU261). Most travellers never claim. Detour drafts it.

      allowed_merchant_categories  MCC allowlist

    100 limit, 110 auth fails).

4. THE RULES (in code - the model never sets a number on its own)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
States: NORMAL -> DISRUPTED -> OVERNIGHT -> REBOOKED -> HOME (closed)

  DISRUPTED  delay >= 2h, cancellation, or missed connection
             MCC 5812 restaurants, 5814 fast food, 4121 taxis,
                 4111 local transport
             limit = city meals rate x days, + 60 EUR taxis
  OVERNIGHT  no departure before 23:00 local, or delay >= 6h
             + MCC 7011 lodging (+ hotel chain codes 3501-3999)
             limit += city lodging rate x 1.2
  REBOOKED   active_to = new departure + 6h; auto_close_date set
  HOME       landed -> INACTIVE, then CLOSED after last captures

City rates: US State Department foreign per diem table (lodging + meals
by city, public) - confirm source and licence before use. Company can
override with its own policy file.

Policy caps (default, per company):
  - 1 night automatic, 2nd night needs a travel manager.
  - Any single raise > 50% of current limit needs a travel manager.
  - Never more than one live Detour card per traveller.
Every approval is bound to: amount, currency, merchant categories, expiry
and the evidence (flight status + airline message) shown to the approver.

What the model does: reads airline notification emails and the status
feed and classifies the disruption; answers the traveller in plain words;
turns requests ("hotel wants a deposit") into a proposed change that the
rules accept or escalate; drafts the EU261 claim. It calls typed tools
only (propose_card_change, request_approval, message_traveller). It never
sees the card number or the API key.

Airwallex rules from the builder guide we follow:
  - request_id on every create; reuse on retry, new one for a new card.
  - Webhooks can arrive twice and out of order: dedupe on event id, sort
    by created_at.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LOOPHOLES AND HOW THEY ARE CLOSED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Traveller spends on non-travel   MCC allowlist on the card itself
Fake disruption to get money     disruption must match the flight
                                 status feed, not just an email
Spoofed airline email            email alone cannot open a card; the
                                 status feed must agree
Card left live after trip        active_to + auto_close_date + close on
                                 landing, three separate guards
Two cards for one disruption     one live card per traveller, request_id
Hotel deposit bigger than limit  incremental auth path; above cap ->
                                 travel manager, never silent raise
Model talked into a big raise    numbers come from rules; model can only
                                 propose
Card in wrong currency           allowed_currencies = local currency

10. APPLICATION TEXT (draft - due 23 Oct)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Detour funds stranded business travellers. When a flight is cancelled or
badly delayed, an agent issues the traveller an Airwallex virtual card in
the local currency, limited to meals, hotels and taxis at that city's
rate, and active only until they fly. As the disruption changes, the
agent changes the card: it adds a hotel when the delay goes overnight,
moves the expiry when they are rebooked, escalates a second night to a
travel manager, and closes the card when they land. Limits, merchant
categories and approvals live in code; the model reads the disruption,
talks to the traveller and proposes changes. Built on Airwallex Issuing
(authorization controls, card updates, simulated authorizations) and
wallets.
