The problem

Travel disruptions
cost more than just
a missed flight.

When a flight is cancelled or badly delayed, employees are left
out of pocket, companies are in the dark, and valuable
compensation goes unclaimed.

Out-of-pocket
costs for travellers

Employees often pay for meals,
taxis and hotels themselves,
usually late at night and in a
foreign country. They keep
receipts, file an expense claim
and wait weeks to be repaid.

The human cost

Invisible disruption costs for companies

no real-time view of what’s
happening, where employees

How it works

From disruption
to support — in
five simple steps.

The agent monitors your flight, detects the disruption and
acts on its own: it pays, adapts and closes, inside your
company’s policy. No forms, no out-of-pocket costs.

  1. 01

    Watch

    We monitor your flight in
    real time, 24/7.

    Detour tracks flight status, schedules
    and changes as they happen.

  2. 02

    Detect

    We identify disruptions
    before you do.

    Cancellations, delays and missed
    connections trigger instant action.

  3. 03

    Fund

    We send you what you need,
    automatically.

    A Detour card for meals and taxis,
    ready on your phone. Hotels are paid directly.

  4. 04

    Adapt

    We adjust as your plans change.

    When flights change, the agent
    changes the money. Anything over
    policy goes to your travel manager.

  5. 05

    Close

    We close the trip and
    claim what the airline owes.

    Unused money never leaves the company.
    The airline claim is written for you.

For companies

Real payment rails, from the first minute of the disruption.

A card with rules

An Airwallex virtual card for restaurants and taxis only. Your policy sets the limit, and it closes when the trip ends.

Hotels paid directly

Each night is paid to the hotel on Airwallex, scheduled for check-in and cancelled if it isn’t needed.

You stay in control

Exceptions wait for your approval. See every payment, your live Airwallex balance and the airline claim.
