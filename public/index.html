How it works

Detour follows the disruption, not the paperwork.

  1. 1

    Watch

    Every trip on file is checked against a live flight status feed and the airline's emails.

  2. 2

    Detect

    A cancellation, a long delay or a missed connection opens cover, before the traveller has landed.

  3. 3

    Fund

    Today's meals and taxis go straight to their bank account, converted to their currency. The hotel is paid directly.

  4. 4

    Adapt

    Rebooked overnight? A hotel is added. Earlier seat? Payments not yet needed are cancelled. Outside policy goes to a manager.

  5. 5

    Close

    When they fly, cover ends. The company gets a summary of every payment, and no one files an expense claim.

Safeguards

An agent that can move money, with the numbers kept out of its hands.

Policy sets every amount

City rates, nights and limits come from rules in code. The model reads emails and talks to the traveller; it can only propose a change.

Hotel money goes to the hotel

The largest cost never passes through the traveller. It is paid to the hotel itself.

Only today is sent

Later days and nights are scheduled, not sent, and are cancelled the moment they are not needed.

An email alone moves nothing

A disruption must match the flight status feed. A forwarded or spoofed email cannot open or raise cover.

Approvals carry the evidence

Anything over policy waits for a travel manager, with the amount, expiry, flight status and airline messages attached.

One cover per trip

Every payment carries a stable request id, so a retry can never pay twice.

Built on Airwallex
